# ⭐ Observation: Sato Bloom versus halo terminology
User clarified Sato visual terminology: 'Bloom' refers to the colored watercolor elements/pigments/petals (green, blue, orange depending on profile), while 'halo' or 'watermark' refers to the pale cream background glow/ring behind the colored bloom. Future UI edits should distinguish these: enlarging the Bloom means increasing colored petal orbit/size/center wash, not enlarging the cream halo/canvas.
