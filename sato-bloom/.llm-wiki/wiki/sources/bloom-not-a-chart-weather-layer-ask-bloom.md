# Bloom reframed: predicted-response portrait, not chart
Critical product reframe from product critique: The Bloom is NOT a chart or data visualization. It paints PREDICTED METABOLIC RESPONSE, not logged inputs. Oats → steady wash. Donut → sharp burst. Pizza → delayed bloom that peaks hours later. Run → green clearing. Added the Weather Layer: 7 weather conditions (calm, clear, foggy, reactive, heavy, restored, charged) replace clinical labels. Added BloomCondition and BLOOM_CONDITIONS types to shared/src/pigments/. Reframed AI chat as Ask Bloom — the AI explains the painting, not a generic chatbot. No visible segmentation — continuous watercolor only, 24-window model is purely internal. The four-layer model is now: Bones (system of record), Skin (app shell), Mind (system of intelligence), Soul (system of reflection/[[sparky-bloom-architecture-synthesis]]). The self-portrait insight: people fall in love with mirrors, not analytics.
