# 🔴 Observation: Bloom reframed as metabolic self-portrait, not chart
Product direction refined: The Bloom is NOT a radial chart or data visualization. It is a living metabolic self-portrait. The goal: open the app and instantly know "today feels turbulent / calm / stable / reactive" without reading any numbers. No visible segmentation — continuous watercolor, no 24-segment clock face, no radial chart appearance. The 24 BloomWindows are purely internal implementation detail. People don't fall in love with analytics; they fall in love with mirrors.
