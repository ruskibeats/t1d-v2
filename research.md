# Research: 90 T1D-Friendly Meal Ideas for SparkyFitness Seed Data

## Summary

A diverse set of 90 meals (30 breakfasts, 30 lunches, 30 dinners) with approximate nutrient profiles for SparkyFitness/T1D educational simulation seed data. Meals are realistic, culturally varied, and balanced — emphasizing whole grains, lean proteins, healthy fats, and fiber rather than extreme restriction. Values are per typical serving and intended for API seed data only, not medical advice.

## Design Principles

- **Balance over restriction**: Meals include complex carbs paired with protein/fat/fiber to model realistic post-meal glucose patterns.
- **Cultural diversity**: Mediterranean, East Asian, South Asian, Latin American, Middle Eastern, African, and North American staples are represented.
- **Fitness-app friendly**: Portions align with active-lifestyle calorie ranges; sodium and sugar values reflect whole-food-centric choices.
- **Educational simulation**: Notes describe why each meal is useful for T1D scenario modeling (e.g., high-fiber, mixed macro, high-GI vs. low-GI contrast).
