# ⭐ Observation: Bloom Impact under every meal entry
The diary needs Bloom Impact under every meal, not just macros. Example: Chicken Salad → "Bloom Impact: Slow release, Stable energy, Minimal disruption". Pepperoni Pizza → "Bloom Impact: Delayed response, Reactive window expected, Recovery ~3 hrs". This is where the T1D prediction engine becomes useful directly in the user's meal logging flow — not as a separate forecast screen, but as contextual insight alongside every entry.
