# ⭐ Observation: Weather Layer concept for Bloom
The Weather Layer reframes metabolic state as weather conditions: Calm, Clear, Foggy, Reactive, Heavy, Restored, Charged. "Today's forecast: Mostly calm morning. Reactive period around lunch. Recovery after exercise." This is memorable — people remember weather, nobody remembers "average glycemic load = 43". The AI becomes Ask Bloom: "Why did today feel heavy? What caused the orange bloom? Why am I tired after lunch? What should I eat before cycling?" — the AI explains the painting, not a generic chatbot.
